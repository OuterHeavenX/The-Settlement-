# THE SETTLEMENT — Production Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Homestead
Focus: first hearth, readable economy, trustworthy saves.
- Expand crop roster and soil states.
- Builder delivery jobs and staged construction art.
- Roads as true navigation weights.
- Citizen needs: hunger, rest, morale.
- IndexedDB chunk/save backend with migrations.
- PixiJS 8 renderer, texture atlases, particles, audio.
- Original building/citizen sprite production pipeline.
- Better wall auto-connection visuals.
- Accessibility and haptics pass.

## Phase 2 — Village
Focus: interdependent production and meaningful population.
- Farmhouse, Chicken Coop, Sheep Pen, Barn, Granary, Mill, Bakery.
- Logs → Planks and Wheat → Flour → Bread chains.
- Market and local prices.
- Expanded citizen schedules and homes.
- School, Well, Tavern.
- Dirt → Gravel road progression.
- Traveling Merchant, Good Harvest, Lost Traveler events.

## Phase 3 — Fortification
Focus: frontier pressure without becoming tower defense.
- Barracks and soldier training.
- Militia, Archers, Guards, Spearmen.
- Patrol routes, gate guards, wall walking.
- Reinforced towers and stone defenses.
- Wolves, raiders, thieves, deserters.
- Security coverage overlay.
- Repairs, equipment, injuries and recovery.
- Expansion requirements based on security + perimeter integrity.

## Phase 4 — Commerce
Focus: regional economy.
- Trading Post, Inn, carts and caravans.
- Trade contracts and regional permits.
- Wool, Leather, Herbs, Cloth, Wine.
- Dynamic demand, market booms and shortages.
- Specialized merchants and logistics workers.
- Prestige and influence as earned civic measures, not arbitrary currencies.

## Phase 5 — Town
Focus: specialized districts.
- Town Hall upgrades.
- Blacksmith, Carpenter, Tailor, Smelter, Workshop.
- Iron Ore → Iron Bars → Tools / Weapons / Armor.
- Clinic and medicine.
- Stone roads and town houses.
- District policies and service coverage.
- Larger contextual enemy groups.

## Phase 6 — Regional Settlement
Focus: large-scale logistics and identity.
- 1,000+ building performance target.
- Simulation LOD tiers for hundreds of citizens.
- Orchards, irrigation, crop rotation, quality and fertility.
- Horses and transport.
- Seasonal clothing and full weather.
- Fairs, festivals, rare traders and regional projects.
- Large territorial claims requiring roads, towers, population, trade and prestige.

## Phase 7 — Endgame Civilization
Focus: years of ownership rather than a finite campaign.
- Reinforced fortifications, ballistae, elite guards.
- Luxury goods and master craftsmen.
- Monumental civic projects.
- Multi-region trade specialization.
- Deep settlement history / chronicle.
- Generational citizens and inherited skills.
- Prestige town layouts and beautification.
- Long-tail research and civilization projects.
- The original homestead remains physically visible at the heart of the grown city.

## Six-Month Territory Principle

Reaching approximately half of the world must come from layered readiness:
security coverage, perimeter construction, roads, population, storage, worker specialization, production chains, civic capacity, permits, prestige and rare trade materials.

Timer inflation must never be the primary gate.
